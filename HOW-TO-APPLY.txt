PATCH 22: moil-reports-period-patch
What it changes
  - Reports page, Blasting log: now follows the top-bar period (last N days, or your custom From/To dates). With "last N days" the upcoming scheduled blasts are still listed. With a custom range only that range is shown.

  - Production summary already followed the period (unchanged).
  - Excel, CSV and PDF downloads follow the same period. No database change.
